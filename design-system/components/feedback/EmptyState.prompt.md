Plannit's most important copy lives here — empty states are the new-user onboarding.

```jsx
<EmptyState icon="users" title="No groups yet"
  body="Groups are how you share the right plans with the right people."
  action={<Button variant="primary" icon="plus">Make a group</Button>} />
```

Never say "You have no items." Say what the thing is for, then offer the one next step.
