Plannit

Find a date that works for everyone

Pick a group, say roughly when, and Plannit checks everyone's calendars and comes back with times that actually work.

  • Nobody sees your calendar Event titles never leave your phone. Your group sees only that you're busy, never what you're doing.
  • One plan, everyone's answer Send a date to the group. Saying yes puts it on your calendar; removing it from your calendar is how you say no.
  • It's your own calendar Plannit works with the calendar already on your iPhone, both ways. Plans you agree to land there like anything else.

Plannit is in beta and not yet on the App Store. If someone sent you an invite link and you don't have the app, hold onto it — it will still work once Plannit is published.

Plannit collects no analytics, shows no ads, and sells nothing about you. What it does store is written out in plain terms in the privacy policy.
